How to Add Products

  1. 📝 Fill in product details (name, category, size, price)
  2. 📤 Pick a photo — it uploads to Cloudinary and fills the URL field for you
  3. ✓ Image preview will appear once the upload finishes
  4. 💾 Click "Add Stock" to save product
